Welcome to Sci-Fi Dojo. We do movie night right. Here's how it works.

A curated physical media rental library built for people who still believe movie night is more than an endless scroll. Sci-fi, fan favorites, cult classics, and hidden gems on Blu-ray, DVD, and 4K UHD. And who knows, we might even have some LaserDiscs and bags of popcorn hiding in there.

The Full Version

"Roads? Where we're going we don't need roads." 2

Hey there, Movie Night Family,

Welcome to Sci-Fi Dojo. This isn't your typical fine print. We're a community built around one simple idea: bringing back the joy of finding and renting a movie for movie night. Remember wandering the aisles of your local video store, picking up a box, reading the back, and feeling that excitement? That's what we're here to recreate.

SFD is a fun-for-everyone project, run with genuine care and fueled by a shared passion for physical media and film. Everything here is built on trust and community. We don't have lawyers. We have popcorn. So in that spirit, here's how we ask everyone to "Do the Right Thing."

When you create your SFD Account and check the box agreeing to these terms, this page is what you're agreeing to. We keep it simple on purpose.

How Your SFD Account Works

"I'm going to make him an offer he can't refuse." 3

Your SFD Account is free. There's no deposit and no monthly fee. You pay per rental, and that's it.

Sign up once with your name and contact info, and you get a personal account link. Bookmark it; it's how you rent, return, and see what you owe. We print SFD cards in batches, so we'll have a physical card with your account's QR code ready for you to pick up on a future visit.

One account per person, and a valid email address is required to use SFD. Your account link is emailed to you when you sign up, reminders and receipts go there, and if you lose your link we'll email it back to the address on your account. Keep your contact info accurate; it's how we reach you about your rentals.

Payments run through Stripe, the same payment platform trusted by millions of businesses. Your card details live with Stripe, not with us.

Pricing & Billing

"Show me the money." 4

Every disc shows its rental price in the app before you pay. The base price covers your first 7 days with the disc.

From day 8, a $2/day extended rental fee is added until the disc is returned, up to the disc's maximum total charge, base price included (more on that below). No late fees, no penalties, just an extended rental fee that costs a little more. Keep it the weekend or keep it two weeks; the app always shows what you've paid and what a rental is accruing.

New Jersey sales tax is added to every charge: the base price, extended rental fees, all of it. The app shows prices before tax; your card statement and Stripe receipts show the tax-included amount.

By renting, you authorize Sci-Fi Dojo to save your payment method with Stripe and to charge it for the base price when you rent, for accrued extended rental fees while a disc is out (charged periodically), and for any remaining balance when the disc is returned, plus applicable sales tax on each charge. This is what lets us skip deposits and monthly fees, so it's the one piece of real fine print here.

When a promo is running, the app shows the promo pricing, and a rental keeps the deal it started under. Prices can change over time; changes apply to new rentals, never to a disc you already have out.

The Maximum Charge

"It's alive! Now please bring it back." 5

Total charges for a rental, base price and extended fees combined, never exceed the disc's listed maximum (plus tax). Once a rental reaches its maximum, the daily fee stops and the disc is yours to keep.

When this happens, we'll also pause new rentals on the account until we connect with you. It's not a penalty, just a chance for us to check in, and we'll usually reach out by email.

That said, we love when a disc finds its way home. Bring it back whenever you're ready, there's no deadline, and we'll work out a partial refund of the extended rental fee with you by email, based on the disc's condition and the situation. We may decline to take it back if we've already replaced it or simply don't need it in the collection anymore, but we'll always talk it through with you rather than leave you guessing.

If you've lost track of a disc, talk to us. We'd always rather sort it out together.

Returns: Two Steps, Both Count

"Houston, we have a problem. Please use the Return Bin." 6

Returning is easy: log the return in your app, then put the disc in the Return Bin. Daily charges stop the moment you log the return, even if we don't check the bin until the next day, and your rental slot frees up right away so you can grab your next movie.

One small rule: returns unlock an hour after checkout. Grabbed the wrong disc and want to swap it right away? Email us and we'll sort it out.

Both steps matter. Logging a return without actually returning the disc isn't a return, and it may mean continued charges or a paused account. If you returned a disc but forgot to log it, tell us and we'll make it right; the goal is accuracy, not gotchas.

Any remaining balance on a rental is charged to your card when we check the disc back in.

If a Charge Fails

"You can't handle the truth, but you can handle updating your card." 7

If a charge to your card fails, new rentals are paused until the balance is settled and the card is updated. Outstanding balances remain due. Your app will show you exactly what's going on, and one email to scifidojo@aol.com gets it fixed.

Rental Limit

"With great rentals comes great responsibility." 8

New accounts rent one disc at a time. Return your rentals on time and your limit grows automatically; that's our way of saying thanks for taking care of the collection. SFD may raise or lower limits at its discretion. If you need more capacity for a movie marathon, just ask.

Your SFD Card & Account Link: Keep Them Close

"Treat it like the One Ring. Keep it secret. Keep it safe." 9

Your SFD card and your account link are unique to your account. The QR code on your card and your personal link both connect directly to it: your rentals, your history, your saved card. Treat them like a password.

Renting for your household movie night? Of course. Just remember it's your account: rentals made with your link or card are your rentals, and charges land on your card. Beyond your immediate household, don't share your card, your QR code, or your link with anyone.

Lost your card? Think your link is compromised? Contact scifidojo@aol.com right away. We can pause your account immediately while a new link and card are issued. Don't wait on this one.

Access & the Honor System

"You shall not pass... without paying first." 10

The general collection is open, no code or key needed. At the cabinet, take only the disc you paid for in the app. Taking anything else is theft, and it's the fastest way to end an account. The honor system is what makes a self-service rental cabinet possible, so please treat it with care.

Damaged & Lost Discs

"I'll be back. And so will this disc, in one piece, please." 11

Stuff happens. Discs get scratched, cases crack, life gets messy. We handle damage and loss case by case. We're not here to nickel and dime anyone.

What we do ask is that you tell us. Flag an issue in the app or reach out at scifidojo@aol.com. Catching a problem early is way more helpful than finding out later, and people who flag damage proactively will always be treated better than people who don't. A disc returned damaged or unplayable may be charged up to its listed maximum, the same cap as a disc that never comes back, but honesty goes a very long way here.

Trade-Ins: Help Us Build the Collection

"Be excellent to each other." 12

Got something we're looking for? We're always working to grow the library, but we're doing it intentionally. Trade-ins are accepted by request only: if there's something specific on our list and you've got it, we want to hear from you, and we'll thank you with free rentals, worked out over email.

All trade-ins are evaluated at SFD's discretion based on condition, format, and current needs. Our Plan A is always to grow the rental library, but traded titles may also go to retail sale or be used in other ways. See something missing from the collection? Tell us. Your request might end up on the list.

A Few House Rules: Taking Care of Our Shared Space

"Do, or do not. There is no try." 13

SFD is a shared space and a shared collection. A little common courtesy goes a long way toward keeping it great for everyone.

Please put things back where you found them. If you pulled something out to look at it, tuck it back in. If you're browsing and something catches your eye, enjoy it. Just return it to its spot when you're done.

Perks like snacks and surprises are cheap on purpose, and they run on an honor box. Prices are marked; drop your payment in the box. The honor bar only works if we can trust it, and it funds the next round of goodies.

SFD may share space with other vendors and neighbors. Please be mindful of other people's areas, displays, and belongings. What's in the SFD cabinets is SFD's. Everything else belongs to someone else. Treat it accordingly.

What We Keep on File

"I know what you rented last summer." 14

We store your name, contact info, and rental history in our own records, and we use them to run the club: tracking rentals, reaching you about a disc, and making the collection better. We don't sell or share your info.

Your payment details are stored and processed by Stripe, not by us. We never see your card number.

The Spirit of It All

"In space, no one can hear you rewind." 15

Sci-Fi Dojo exists because we love movies, we love physical media, and we love the ritual of movie night. Every policy above is written in that spirit. We're not trying to catch anyone out. We're trying to build something fun and sustainable that everyone can enjoy.

If something feels off, talk to us. If you're going through something, talk to us. If you just want to geek out about the collection, absolutely talk to us. That's what this is for.

Now go pick something good.
