The Full Version

"Roads? Where we're going we don't need roads." 2

Hey there, Movie Night Family,

Welcome to Sci-Fi Dojo. This isn't your typical fine print. We're a community built around one simple idea: bringing back the pure joy of renting a movie for movie night. Remember wandering the aisles of your local video store, picking up a box, reading the back, and feeling that excitement? That's what we're here to recreate.

SFD is a fun-for-everyone project, run with love and fueled by a shared passion for physical media and the sci-fi genre. Everything here is built on trust and community. We don't have lawyers. We have popcorn. So in that spirit, here's how we ask everyone to "Do the Right Thing."

How the Membership Works

"I'm going to make him an offer he can't refuse." 3

SFD uses Stripe for membership billing, the same payment platform trusted by millions of businesses. Secure, reliable, and it works with whatever card you already have.

Your membership is billed on a rolling 30-day cycle starting from your signup date. Your first payment includes two separate line items: your deposit and your first month's membership fee. After that, only the monthly fee recurs. No contracts, no runaround. Cancel anytime, come back just as easily.

Your disc limit, loan window, current rentals, and due dates are always available in your member app at scifidojo.com/member. That's your source of truth for everything specific to your plan.

If you decide to cancel, please let us know directly at scifidojo@aol.com so we can take care of your deposit and make sure everything is wrapped up properly.

Deposits

"With great rentals comes great responsibility." 4

Your deposit is collected at signup via Stripe as a clearly labeled line item on your first payment, separate from your membership fee so there's never any confusion about what you paid. It's held as a good faith gesture, not as a punishment. We trust you. The deposit is just our way of making sure everyone takes care of the collection we've all built together.

When you're ready to move on, we'll miss you. Here's how deposits work on the way out:

  • Cancel your Stripe subscription AND let us know directly at scifidojo@aol.com
  • All discs must be returned before we start the clock on your refund
  • Your refund window begins after your billing period has fully expired, not just your cancellation date. On a rolling subscription you retain access until the end of your current cycle, so we wait until that period ends and everything is back on the shelf
  • Refunds are issued within 30 days of that date, back to your original payment method via Stripe
  • If your card on file is no longer active, we'll arrange your refund via Venmo. Just reach out to scifidojo@aol.com
  • Members must be in good standing to receive a full refund
  • Sci-Fi Dojo reserves the right to suspend or terminate memberships in cases of misuse
How Rentals Work

"It's alive! Now please return it by the due date." 5

Renting is easy. Scan the QR code on your member card or open your member link; your info is already there. Enter the disc code from the rental sticker, select checkout, and submit. Returns work exactly the same way.

Your member app shows your current rentals, due dates, and everything specific to your membership. Check it anytime at scifidojo.com/member.

If something runs long, just let us know. We'll work with you. If things stay out too long, we may pause access until everything is back.

Every rental item has a rental sticker on it. That sticker means the item belongs to the lending library. Please don't remove it. Items without a rental sticker are available for sale at the register.

There's a notes field on every transaction and we want you to use it. Write a quick review. Tell us about a movie it reminded you of. Share a fun fact about the director. Recommend something we should add to the collection. Report an issue with a disc. Or just tell us you loved it. We read every single one, and who knows, your note might end up in our next newsletter or your pick might make it onto our Member's Picks display.

Oh, and if snacks, drinks, or a surprise are out when you come in, don't forget to grab yours. First come, first served, and they go fast.

Your Member Card & Member Link: Keep Them Close

"You can't handle the truth — but you can handle the rental code." 6

Your member card and your member link are unique to your membership. The QR code on your card and your personal member link both connect directly to your account: your rental history, your disc limit, your due dates. Treat them like a password.

Immediate family members are welcome to use your card and link. Just let us know who they are and make sure they know the process. They're an extension of your membership, which means they're an extension of your responsibility. If something goes sideways, it's on your account.

Beyond immediate family, please do not share your card, your QR code, or your member link with anyone else. Sharing them gives someone else full access to your membership, your rental slots, and your account history. If something goes wrong under your account, it's on your account.

Lost your card? Think your link might be compromised? Contact scifidojo@aol.com right away. We can lock your membership immediately while a new card and member link are issued. Don't wait on this one.

The Cabinet Code: Your Membership, Your Responsibility

"Houston, we have a problem. Please use the notes field." 7

Your monthly cabinet code is how you access the collection. It goes out in the newsletter each month and is tied to active memberships only.

Immediate family members are welcome to use your code. Beyond that, please don't share it. This is one of the few things that can get a membership revoked. The code keeps the collection secure and accessible only to our community. Please treat it that way.

Want to rent something for a friend, a date night, a family gathering? Go for it. That's the whole spirit of this place. Just remember: it's your membership, you're in charge of that rental, and you're responsible for getting it back. Get it? Got it? Good.

Damaged & Lost Discs

"I'll be back. And so will this disc — in one piece, please." 8

Stuff happens. Discs get scratched, cases crack, life gets messy. We handle damage and loss on a case by case basis. We're not here to nickel and dime anyone.

What we do ask is that you tell us. Use the notes field when you check a disc back in, or reach out directly at scifidojo@aol.com. Catching a problem early is way more helpful than finding out later. Members who flag damage proactively will always be treated better than those who don't. There's a dedicated drop-off spot for discs that might need a clean or some attention. Please use it.

Hold Policy

"You had me at hello." 9

Want to make sure something is waiting for you? Members can place holds on available titles. Your hold window is based on your membership tier. Check your member app at scifidojo.com/member for your specific hold period.

Holds are first come, first served and subject to availability. If a title already has a hold on it, we can't place another one. Holds are a courtesy, not a guarantee, but we'll always do our best to make it work.

Trade-Ins: Help Us Build the Collection

"Be excellent to each other." 10

Got something we're looking for? We're always working to grow the library, but we're doing it intentionally.

Trade-ins are accepted by request only. If there's something specific we're looking for and you've got it, we want to hear from you.

General Requests

Titles from our active request list count toward rewards. Ten requested titles earns one free month.

Rare / Out-of-Print

Hard-to-find, out-of-print, or highly requested titles may qualify for a full month on their own.

These are our baseline rewards. Exceptional titles, formats, or condition may earn more.

All trade-ins are evaluated at SFD's discretion based on condition, format, and current needs.

Our Plan A is always to grow the rental library. Traded titles may also go to retail sale, be held for evaluation, or be used in other ways at SFD's discretion. We can't guarantee every trade-in will end up available for rent, but growing the collection is always the goal.

See something missing from the collection? Tell us. Your request might end up on the list.

A Few House Rules: Taking Care of Our Shared Space

"Do, or do not. There is no try." 11

SFD is a shared space and a shared collection. A little common courtesy goes a long way toward keeping it great for everyone.

Please put things back where you found them. If you pulled something out to look at it, tuck it back in. If you're browsing and something catches your eye, enjoy it. Just return it to its spot when you're done.

We know life moves fast. If you're in a rush and need to return a disc but don't have time to find its exact spot, every cabinet has a designated return section. Just pop it in there and we'll get it sorted. Please don't leave returns on top of the cabinet, on nearby surfaces, or in someone else's space.

SFD may share space with other vendors and neighbors. Please be mindful of other people's areas, displays, and belongings. What's in the SFD cabinets is SFD's. Everything else belongs to someone else. Treat it accordingly.

The Spirit of It All

"In space, no one can hear you rewind." 12

Sci-Fi Dojo exists because we love movies, we love physical media, and we love the ritual of movie night. Every policy above is written in that spirit. We're not trying to catch anyone out. We're trying to build something fun and sustainable that everyone can enjoy.

If something feels off, talk to us. If you're going through something, talk to us. If you just want to geek out about the collection, absolutely talk to us. That's what this is for.

Now go pick something good.

"Do the right thing." 13
