# Everything is crawlable on purpose. /rent, /terms and /member carry
# their own <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> instead of being
# blocked here -- deliberately, because the two are not interchangeable:
# a page blocked in robots.txt cannot be fetched at all, so Google never
# reads the noindex tag and may still list the bare URL if it finds a
# link to it somewhere. Letting crawlers in is what makes the noindex
# actually stick.
